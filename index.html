Executive summary

Michael Phelps came into the Rio Olympics as the most decorated Olympian in history with 18 gold medals. He won two more golds on the first night. After the second race, still catching his breath, he looked ahead to his remaining events and said:

“I’m not even halfway done yet.”

That’s how September felt for the ReLo SEs. We have some wins, some gold medals:

  • The largest deal in Redo history went live (Grailed).
  • Highest ever M0 deal attainment at 34%.
  • Hiring and headcount held us back for a long time. That’s no longer an issue. It’s a strength. Headcount is full, with more signed offers lined up over the next few months.

I can easily shout out Frank Marino, Parker Mackay and Seth Weeks for wins in their motions. More details below.

At the same time, we’re not even halfway done yet. We have so much more value to drive for the business and huge levers to take us to the next level. And, frankly, these are simple fixes. Not always easy, but simple.

Here’s a deeper breakdown of our “gold medals” and our “not-even-halfway-done-yet’s.”

Gold medals

1

Enterprise onboardings

“I could never play him the same way twice, because I knew that it just wasn’t going to work.” Bill Russell, on playing against Wilt Chamberlain.

Our first enterprise deals were very rocky. Marc Jacobs took us 60 days from signature to go-live. Seth is channeling that Wilt energy: learning from every deal, documenting, communicating, improving. Every deal has gotten smoother. Grailed took us 40 days at 2x the complexity. We have an experienced hire starting Nov 2 to level us up even further.

I’d be remiss not to shout out Jesse and the rest of our engineering partners for their critical role in this improvement.

2

RMR SEs

Frank is a new leader with a brand new team averaging 1 month of tenure. They held churn to $0 while hitting $1.2M in new business. This came after we lost nearly all of our tribal knowledge at the end of August.

3

New incentives and pipeline visibility

Team incentives and culture have real momentum. Previously, SEs were only measured on actualized revenue. Very aligned incentives, but less dopamine. That meant less urgency to get deals live by EOM, to push for contract go-lives, and to attack and prevent churn.

The combination of the newly launched Net New Revenue quota (deal size of deals live) and the existing Deal Attainment is driving the right behavior. Our leaderboard now includes a funnel of the job: inputs → levers → outcomes. The winner across all 3 is the Top Dog.

Shoutout to August Qvist for edging out Lauren in the final hour and taking the crown.

4

International

Parker and team are cooking. They’re hiring great people and building a mini post-sales function complete with on-site SEs, AMs and support. He did this all while hitting 307% of his quota, might I add.

5

Onboarding churn

We ended with $307K of M0 to M2 churn, 1.7% of booked deal size. That’s down from 2.5% in August, largely due to the shift in incentives.

“We’re not even halfway done yet”

1

Contracts

  • On-time go-lives. If all the September contract go-lives had gone live on schedule, our attainment would’ve jumped from 70.3% to 75.1%. The majority of that 4.8% was Chicks Saddlery (2.7%) and Darc Sport (1%).
  • More consistency. 62% of contracts are going live on time right now. We’re up from 50% in July, but this needs to be 80%+.
2

Training

I’ve been the bottleneck here without a manager structure. We need to treat and train each segment differently:

  • SMB needs consistent training, performance management and clarity. New hire M0 deal attainment was 17% compared to 34% for ramped SMB reps.
  • Growth needs more technical and integrations enablement to speed up time to value. We’re seeing a 9% attainment drop for newly promoted Growth reps in their first month.
  • Mid-market needs project management and tech enablement that will boost the contract numbers above.
How we’re addressing it

Craig as SMB team lead. Parker as international team lead. Seth over custom. Hugh over Growth and Mid-market.

3

AE to SE relationship

It’s “fine.” It needs to be excellent. AEs are our most important partners.

How we’re addressing it

Joint forecasting, joint trainings by segment, joint activities, and sales-minded leadership (Craig, new Solution Architect starting in Nov).

4

RMR deal attainment

Big room for improvement here. Before Oct, we only measured ourselves on new business because the only priority was growth. That’s hurting us on deal attainment. Going forward we’re mirroring the Returns SE incentives.
