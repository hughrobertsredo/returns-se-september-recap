Executive summary

The Returns SE book ran at 70.3% of deal size across M0, M1, and M2. Our benchmark of success is 70% attainment, which means we’re attaining at 30% of M0, 85% of M1, and 100% of M2 deal size.

We saw $12.80M of run rate on $18.22M of deal size, 794 deals across the 3 months, 12 ramped SEs. Best month since April and up 14% from August.

Run rate / deal size is the SE key metric and the primary measure throughout the report. A healthy deal meets ramp: 30% of deal size in month 0, 85% in month 1, 100% in month 2. Every point below or above is measured against that baseline.

Highlights

  • We hit target. 119.9% of net live (157% including Grailed), 106.5% of revenue quota. This translates to 70.3% deal attainment across M0, M1, and M2. $12.80M of run rate is an all-time high.
  • Contracts: Seth onboarded the largest deal in Redo history. Grailed went live at $2.0M of deal size and is expected to perform at $6M.
  • Month 0: 34% attainment is the highest in 12+ months. More urgency = more deals live sooner.
  • Live non-contract deals: 8.2% above target. Month 1 was 116% vs. the 85% benchmark.
  • Run rate: $12.80M, an all-time high.
